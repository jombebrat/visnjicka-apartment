House rules.

A few simple things to keep the apartment — and the building — happy.

🕒

Check-in & out

Check-in: from 15:00
Check-out: by 11:00

Early check-in or late check-out may be possible — please contact the host in advance.

🚭

No Smoking

Smoking is strictly forbidden anywhere inside the apartment or building — including cigarettes and e-cigarettes. Violation will result in additional cleaning fees.

🗑️

Waste

Do not use the bins inside the building. All waste must be disposed of in the outdoor containers just outside the building entrance. Thank you for respecting your neighbours.

🔇

Neighbours

Please be mindful, especially between 22:00 and 08:00. Keep music and TV at a reasonable volume. This is a residential building — treat it as your own home.

🐾

Pets

Pets are not allowed without prior approval from the host. Please contact us before booking if you are travelling with a pet.

👥

Guests & Parties

The apartment is for registered guests only. Unregistered visitors are not permitted to stay overnight. Parties and events are not allowed.

Getting around

Local transport.

Belgrade is one of the easiest European capitals to navigate — and as of 2025, public transport is completely free.

🚌

Public transport — free

Since January 2025, all buses, trams, trolleybuses and BG:Voz trains are completely free. Just hop on. Bus stop "Vuka Vrčevića" is 2 min away — line 16 goes to the centre in ~15 min.

📱

Taxi apps

CarGo is the main local taxi app — reliable, with the price shown upfront. Most rides within the city cost 300–600 RSD. Always preferred over flagging a taxi on the street.

🚲

Bike & scooter

NextBike bike sharing has stations across the city. Electric scooters (Bolt, Lime) are widely available too. Ideal for Kalemegdan fortress and the riverfront.

Right next door

Big Fashion shopping mall.

One of Belgrade's biggest malls is practically on your doorstep — a 10-minute walk along Višnjička, or one bus stop on lines 16, 23, or 25.

🛍️

Shopping

Over 100 stores including Zara, H&M, Reserved, Pull&Bear, Bershka, Mango, New Yorker, Sport Vision and more. Open daily 10:00–22:00.

🍽️

Food & cafés

Large food court on the top floor with fast food and sit-down options. Kafeterija specialty coffee inside is excellent. IDEA supermarket on the lower level — open 9:00–22:00.

🎬

Entertainment

Cinema, bookstores, game stores and a kids play area. Lidl is just next door (Višnjička 94), and Maxi is a 5 min walk behind the mall.

🍷

Ada Huja riverfront (5 min walk): Right behind Big Fashion, along the Danube, you'll find Caffe Restaurant X and Caffe Dilema — both rated 4.4–4.5★, with great food and beautiful river views. Perfect for lunch, dinner, or evening drinks. A true local gem most tourists miss.

Food & Drink

Where to eat & drink.

From traditional kafanas to specialty coffee, here's how to eat like a local.

🥩

Serbian classics

Don't miss ćevapi (grilled minced meat), pljeskavica (Serbian burger), and burek (flaky pastry). Skadarlija has the best traditional kafanas — try Tri Šešira or Dva Jelena.

☕

Coffee culture

Belgrade has a vibrant café scene. Savamala is the creative hub with specialty coffee. Locally, try the cafés along Ada Huja riverbank — great views, 5 min from the apartment.

🍺

Nightlife

Belgrade is famous for nightlife. Savamala for bars and clubs, Skadarlija for bohemian. River clubs ("splavovi") on the Sava are uniquely Belgrade. Most places stay open very late.

🛒

Groceries nearby

Maxi is the closest large store — about 10 min walk. Idea and Lidl are also nearby. For fresh produce, the Karaburma pijaca market is a great local experience.

🌅

Ada Huja

Your closest riverside escape. Outdoor cafés, a go-kart track, tennis courts, and a beach area in summer. Perfect for a morning walk or afternoon coffee. Locals love it.

💡

Good to know

Tipping is common — 10% is standard. Most places accept cards but smaller kafanas may be cash only. Lunch is typically 12–15h, dinner from 19h onwards.

Sightseeing

What to see & do.

A handpicked list — from icons to local secrets.

  • 🏰
    Kalemegdan FortressBelgrade's most iconic landmark. Free entry, stunning views over the Danube and Sava. Best at sunset. ~20 min by bus.
  • 🎭
    SkadarlijaThe bohemian quarter. Cobblestone street lined with kafanas, live music, and street artists. Best in the evening.
  • 🚶
    Knez MihailovaThe main pedestrian street. Shopping, cafés, performers — a free open-air museum of 19th century architecture.
  • 🎨
    SavamalaThe creative hub. Street art, galleries, design studios, and the best bars in the city. Comes alive on weekends.
  • ⛪
    Saint Sava TempleOne of the largest Orthodox churches in the world. Impressive interior, free entry.
  • 🏊
    Ada CiganlijaBelgrade's river island beach. Swimming, cycling, sports in summer. Very popular with locals.
  • 🏛️
    National MuseumReopened after renovation, with an excellent collection of Serbian and European art. Republic Square.
  • 🌿
    Topčider ParkBeautiful 19th century park. Horse stables, cafés, peaceful walks.
  • 🌉
    Brankov Bridge walkWalk across for great views of both rivers meeting. Best at golden hour.
  • 🎪
    ZemunHistoric town now part of Belgrade. Austro-Hungarian architecture, riverside promenade, excellent fish restaurants.
Practical info

Good to know.

Small details that make a big difference.

💳

Money & payments

Currency is Serbian Dinar (RSD). 1€ ≈ 117 RSD. Exchange at banks or official offices — avoid hotel exchanges. Cards accepted almost everywhere.

📶

Internet & SIM

Apartment WiFi is 300 Mbps — more than enough for video calls and streaming. Prepaid SIMs from Telenor or A1: 10GB for ~500 RSD.

🏥

Health & safety

Belgrade is generally very safe. Normal city precautions apply. Nearest pharmacy: within 5 min walk. Emergency: 194.

🗣️

Language

Serbian is official. English is widely spoken in restaurants, shops, and by younger generations. Words locals appreciate: Hvala (Thank you), Molim (Please).

🌤️

Weather

Spring: 15–22°C. Summer: 25–38°C. Autumn: 15–25°C. Winter: 0–8°C. The apartment has AC and heating.

🕐

Belgrade timing

Belgrade runs on its own schedule — lunch is 13–15h, dinner rarely before 20h. Nightlife starts at midnight. Shops open ~10h, often Sundays too.
