Before you leave

No smoking reminder

If smoking occurred inside the apartment or building, additional cleaning fees will apply. Thank you for respecting the rules.

Please do not use the bins inside the building.

Check-out checklist

  1. 1

    Collect all your belongings

    Check all rooms, wardrobes, bathroom, and the balcony. Don't forget chargers, toiletries, and items left to dry.

  2. 2

    Garbage (optional)

    If you'd like to dispose of any waste, the outdoor containers are right next to the building. No obligation: cleaning is included.

  3. 3

    Turn off all lights & appliances

    Switch off all lights, the TV, and AC. Make sure the stove and oven are off.

  4. 4

    Close all windows & doors

    Close and lock all windows. Lock the apartment door behind you.

  5. 5

    Return the key to the lockbox

    Place the apartment key back into the lockbox on mailbox nr. 1 and return the entry tag to the lockbox at the building entrance. Leave both lockboxes properly closed. After closing, spin the dials to a random combination.

  6. That's it. Safe travels!
