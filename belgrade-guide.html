From the airport

Get a taxi voucher first

After baggage claim, find the official taxi voucher desk in the arrivals hall. The voucher fixes your fare, so you can't be overcharged. Hand it to the driver at the official taxi queue outside.

Official taxi with voucherAbout 30 min3 000–3 500 RSDabout €26–30
Minibus A1 via SlavijaAbout 50 min400 RSDabout €3.50
Rental carAbout 30 minVaries
Don't accept offered rides

Ignore anyone who approaches you inside the terminal offering a ride. Use only the official taxi queue with your voucher, where prices are fixed.

SIM card

Telenor and A1 kiosks are right after baggage claim in the arrivals hall. Both have reliable coverage across Serbia.

About €5–6 for 10 GBA prepaid SIM costs around 500–700 RSD. Top up at any kiosk (trafika) in the city.
Bring your passportRegistration at the counter takes a few minutes.
Coming from the EU?Serbia isn't in the EU, so roaming can be expensive. A local SIM is the better choice for longer stays.
CoverageExcellent 4G across the country, and 5G in central Belgrade.
Landing late?If the kiosks are closed, buy a SIM the next morning at any phone shop or large supermarket.
300 Mbps Wi-Fi at homeFast enough for video calls and streaming, so you may not need much mobile data.

Getting around

Public transport is freeSince January 2025, all buses, trams, trolleybuses and BG:Voz trains are free for everyone. Just hop on.
CarGo taxi appThe main local taxi app, with the price shown upfront. Most city rides cost 300–600 RSD. Always better than hailing a taxi on the street.
Bikes and scootersNextBike stations around the city, plus Bolt and Lime e-scooters. Great for Kalemegdan and the riverfront.

Coffee and nights out

Coffee cultureBelgrade has a vibrant café scene, and Savamala is the creative hub for specialty coffee. Closer to home, try the cafés along the Ada Huja riverbank, 5 min from the apartment.
NightlifeSavamala for bars and clubs, Skadarlija for a bohemian night out. The river clubs (splavovi) on the Sava are uniquely Belgrade. Most places open late and stay open very late.
Ada Huja, 5 min awayYour closest riverside escape: outdoor cafés, a go-kart track, tennis courts and a beach in summer. Locals love it for a morning walk or an afternoon coffee.

Good to know

TippingCommon, and 10% is standard.
Cards or cashMost places accept cards, but smaller kafanas may be cash only.
Meal timesLunch is usually 12:00–15:00 and dinner from 19:00 onwards. Restaurants stay open late.

House rules

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00. Early check-in or late check-out may be possible, so please ask the host in advance.

No smoking

Smoking is strictly forbidden anywhere inside the apartment or the building. This includes e-cigarettes and any other smoking products. Smoking will result in an additional cleaning fee.

Waste and garbage

Please don't use the bins inside the building. Take all waste to the outdoor containers just outside the entrance. Thank you for respecting your neighbours.

Noise and neighbours

Please be mindful of neighbours, especially between 22:00 and 08:00. Keep music and TV at a reasonable volume. This is a residential building, so treat it as your own home.

Pets

Pets aren't allowed without the host's approval. Please get in touch before booking if you're travelling with a pet.

Guests and parties

The apartment is for registered guests only. Unregistered visitors can't stay overnight, and parties or events aren't allowed.
